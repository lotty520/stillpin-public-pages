Privacy Policy

Effective date November 12, 2026
Publisher Stillpin

Stillpin helps you compare similar photos and choose which ones to keep. Photo grouping and analysis run on your device. The app has no Stillpin account, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated photo upload service.

Photos and permissions

You can use the iOS photo picker to provide only the photos you choose. If you ask Stillpin to browse by date or album, add picks to an album, or clean up selected photos, it may request access to Photos. You can review or change that access in iOS Settings. If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it through Apple’s Photos services.

What Stillpin processes and keeps

Stillpin uses selected photo content, identifiers and available metadata, such as capture time, to compare and group photos. It may compute sharpness, detect faces and compare face features across photos in a review. These operations happen locally. Stillpin does not maintain a face identity library or send photos, face features or analysis results to the publisher.

The app stores session records, photo references, selection decisions and cached analysis in its local app storage so you can resume work. It also stores your app language preference locally. Photos provided through the system picker are copied into local app storage, including the paired video for Live Photos, so they remain available when you reopen the app. Thumbnails may be cached in memory. To prepare a share, Stillpin creates temporary copies of photo resources and attempts to remove them when the share flow ends or on the next launch after an interruption. App records and imported copies are kept while the app’s local data is retained. To remove the local app data from the device, choose Delete App in iOS. Photos and exports saved outside the app, and any copies in backups, are managed separately.

Sharing, albums and cleanup

You decide whether to share or save a selection. If you choose a destination through the iOS share sheet, that destination may receive photo files and their metadata. Its privacy practices apply to what it receives. Adding picks to Photos uses Apple’s Photos services. Stillpin requests deletion of original library items only after you explicitly choose cleanup and confirm through iOS.

Purchases and support

If you start or restore the free trial, or buy or restore Pro, Apple processes the transaction and any payment. Stillpin reads the StoreKit purchase and entitlement information needed to verify the trial or unlock Pro. The publisher does not receive your payment card details through the app.

If you contact the publisher, the publisher uses the contact details and information you voluntarily provide to respond and resolve your request, and retains them as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending private photos or unnecessary personal information.

Your choices and contact

For questions or applicable privacy requests about information you sent to the publisher, email developer@tjxrkj.com. If the policy changes, the revised text and effective date will appear on this page.
