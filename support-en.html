Support

Questions, feedback or trouble choosing your photos? Email the Stillpin developer.

developer@tjxrkj.com

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Stillpin version and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send private photos, passwords or payment details. If you attach a screenshot, remove personal information first.

Common questions

Do I need to grant access to my whole library?

No. Start with the system photo picker and provide just the photos you choose. Features such as browsing by date or album, album export and library cleanup request Photos access when needed.

Does Stillpin upload my photos?

Photo grouping and analysis run locally. Stillpin does not send photos or face features to the developer. iOS may download iCloud originals, Apple handles purchases, and sharing sends files only to your chosen destination.

Does choosing a photo delete the others?

No. Selection is saved within the batch. Deleting library originals is a separate, optional action that requires iOS confirmation. With iCloud Photos, deletion may sync to your other devices.

A photo will not load. What can I try?

Check Photos access in iOS Settings, available device storage and your network connection if originals are in iCloud. Return to the batch and retry the failed item.

How do the trial and Pro work?

The 30-day trial starts when you actively claim it. It does not renew or charge automatically. After it ends, buy Pro once to start new batches. Previously admitted work can finish and existing batches stay available for review and export.

How do I restore a purchase or request a refund?

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen with the Apple Account used for the original trial or Pro purchase. Restoring does not restart the trial. For refund requests, use Apple’s refund service.
